Changes: GeoPackage WGS84 polygons were exported to GeoJSON and grouped into
49 spatial download shards, without clipping, simplification, coordinate
rounding, reprojection, union, overlap repair or attribute editing. Each of
2,980 complete source records appears exactly once. All 514,392 coordinates
and seven attributes are preserved. Index bounding boxes select downloads;
they are never used as map coverage. Geometry and source-attribute equality
were checked against an independent whole-dataset export.

The collection is the 1500 cross-section. Three records (106,2477,2478) have
null TIME_START/TIME_END and retain these nulls. QUALITY is unfilled in 2,881
records; this is absence of a stated basis, not proof of high accuracy. Three
source overlap pairs exceed 1 square metre and remain unchanged. Display is
stable in ascending fid order; this order does not resolve conflicting claims.
The map shows all hit records in its centre-point landscape reader.

Source class codes are 0,1,2,3,4,7,8,9. Class 0 includes cultivatable,
residential and agricultural land. Class 7 includes fresh OR coastal water.
Navigability categories preserve source descriptions and are not depth or
vessel-route measurements. Modern terrain remains geographic context; these
polygons contain no historical elevation measurements. Landscape fid and
locality fid are independent identifiers and are not joined by equality.
