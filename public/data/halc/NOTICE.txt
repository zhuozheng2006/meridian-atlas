Coverage and meaning / 覆盖范围与含义
This layer contains the source reconstruction for 1500 of the Low Countries and
adjacent northern France and western Germany. Bounds (WGS84): 0.5394290755 to
8.3519884661 degrees east, 48.5402029861 to 54.0243305418 degrees north. The data
describe local administrative/judicial, combined religious-administrative and
natural units. They are not merged into national outlines. The Alps, Switzerland
and Bavaria are outside this layer's coverage.

Source TIME_START=1500-01-01 and TIME_END=1500-12-31 identify this reconstruction
snapshot, not exact historical locality lifetimes. NAME preserves the source's
modern normalized spelling. NAME_EXT and some ADM8/ADM9 values contain later
periods' comparison labels and remain only in detailed source attributes.
Complete semicolon memberships retain their original order for shared or
contested affiliation. XX groups small sovereign regions; it is not one state.
TYPE 8 identifies natural features. QUALITY records the basis of reconstruction,
including modern-derived or estimated boundaries, not a numeric accuracy scale.

Changes made for this atlas / 本图集进行的转换
Original WGS84 polygons were projected to Web Mercator and quantized to a shared
integer grid separately at zoom levels 6–10 (MVT extent 8192). Each polygon was
clipped to tile bounds with a 64-unit buffer using the same grid. Quantization
can collapse very small parts. Exact collinear redundancies were removed at zero
tolerance, with topological equality checked after quantization. Ring winding
was encoded for MVT. There was no geographic smoothing, manual boundary repair,
historical interpolation or ADM1 union. The original GeoPackage is unchanged
and remains available from the official source above.

All 14,863 original localities and all 55 non-geometry attributes are preserved
in 59 compressed detail shards. Original fid values identify both tile features
and detail records within source version 15.0. There are 814 uncompressed MVT
tiles, including 400 valid empty tiles; every tile inside the source's per-zoom
rectangular range is provided. At z6, six tiny localities have no encoded polygon:
fid 1970, 7010, 10002, 11961, 14141 and 14160. All 14,863 appear at z7–10 and in
details. Zooming beyond z10 enlarges those tiles without adding source detail.
Map properties are fid, NAME, SHORT_ID, ADM0, ADM1, TYPE and QUALITY. The remaining
source attributes, 68 region definitions, administrative dictionaries and 246
bibliographic entries are retained. Chinese legend labels are editorial
translations; original codebook definitions remain available.

本层提供1500年低地国家及邻接地区的地方单位重建，覆盖范围不含阿尔卑斯、
瑞士和巴伐利亚。地名采用来源的现代规范拼写，共有或争议归属及其顺序完整
